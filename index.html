WIN4SMEs

About the WIN4SMEs Project

WIN4SMEs is a European development initiative that brings together vocational education providers, higher education institutions, and business support organizations to strengthen workplace innovation in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). SMEs make up 99% of all businesses in the EU and provide around 70% of all jobs.

With 20 partners from 9 countries, WIN4SMEs is establishing Centres of Vocational Excellence (CoVEs) across Europe, building a broad learning network that merges research, practical skills, and business development expertise.

Process

Selection Methodology

13 practices selected through a rigorous 4-step evaluation process

1

Understanding WPI

Preliminary data collection from all partners on how they define workplace innovation.

2

Collecting Practices

78 best practices submitted by partners via e-form and compiled in a structured template.

3

Pre-evaluation

25 practices shortlisted by an independent evaluation team based on agreed criteria.

4

Final Selection

13 practices chosen during a workshop in Kolding, Denmark through collaborative voting.
